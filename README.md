## 快速预览：3 个高价值提示词

**1. 选题**
> "我是一名[专业]方向研究生，研究方向[X]。结合[Y]领域近期进展，提出5个研究问题：①有创新性 ②6个月可完成 ③结果可量化。说明每个问题的研究价值和所需数据。"

**2. 降 AI 味**
> "Rewrite the following to sound human: reduce passive voice, vary sentence length, add field-specific transitions, remove formulaic phrases. Keep all technical content. [text]"

**3. 投稿信**
> "Draft a cover letter for [journal]: highlight novelty of [X], fit with journal scope, co-author approval, not under consideration elsewhere. Under 250 words."
